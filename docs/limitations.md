# Limitations

- Administrative boundaries are operational datasets and are not automatically
  equivalent to legally authoritative survey boundaries.
- WorldPop values are modelled estimates, not census enumeration counts.
- Fine pixel size does not imply equivalent local accuracy.
- Settlement extents are mapped settlement extents, not official community
  boundaries.
- Settlement names and settlement polygons are separate products and may not
  map one-to-one.
- Building detections can contain omission, commission, positional and temporal
  errors.
- A detected building is not necessarily residential or occupied.
- GRID3 health-facility data are operational/non-exhaustive and can contain
  temporal or positional inconsistencies.
- Settlement populations need not sum to the LGA total because population may
  be modelled outside mapped settlement extents.
